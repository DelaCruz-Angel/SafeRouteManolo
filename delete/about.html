About SafeRouteManolo

Who We Are

SafeRouteManolo is a website dedicated to providing real-time updates on hazardous areas along Manolo routes. We aim to help drivers, commuters, and pedestrians stay informed and safe while navigating roads.

Features

View recent hazards, charts, and live weather on our interactive dashboard.

Explore interactive maps with hazardous locations marked for your safety.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make roads safer by providing accurate and timely information about hazardous areas. We empower the community to report issues and stay informed, reducing accidents and promoting road safety awareness.
